## Information we process

Depending on configuration and use, we may process:

- Agency account details, user credentials, settings, and audit events.
- Property listings, inquiry details, caller phone numbers, lead qualifications, appointments, and follow-up status.
- Call audio streams while a call is active, transcripts and conversation turns where enabled, tool requests and results, call timing, and service diagnostics.
- Consent records supplied or captured by the agency, and technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

Do not send information the service is not designed to handle, including payment-card authentication data or sensitive personal data, unless separately agreed.

## Purposes and roles

We process information to provide and secure the service, route calls, respond to inquiries, maintain agency records, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. The agency customer generally decides why caller and lead information is collected and is responsible for its notices, lawful basis, consent, and call-recording choices. Propview processes that information on the customer's instructions as described in the Data Processing Agreement. Propview acts independently for account administration, security, and service operations where applicable law permits.
